Meet Marisa

With decades of experience in reading education and a heart for every learner, Marisa founded this practice on one belief: no child should feel left behind when they open a book.

Marisa's Reading Chair isn't a franchise or a learning center. It's a private, expert-led practice where your child receives the undivided attention of a specialist who has devoted her career to the science—and soul—of reading.

  • Orton-Gillingham trained Reading Specialist
  • Decades of classroom and intervention experience
  • Personalized, 1:1 instruction in McKinney, TX
  • Faith-integrated, whole-child philosophy

Science & Spirit

Clinical excellence meets a grandmother's heart. Our approach weaves evidence-based reading science with a faith-grounded commitment to every child's potential.

The Expert Lens

Using the Science of Reading—multisensory, evidence-based methods—to identify and bridge the specific gaps in your child's literacy profile. No guesswork. No one-size-fits-all.

The Spiritual Anchor

A "Whole Child" approach that views your student as a masterpiece, building confidence through encouragement and prayerful mentorship. We nurture the reader and the person.

Visible Victory

Data-driven progress tracking. You'll see exactly how your child is moving from "struggling" to "fluent" on our Mastery Dashboard. Real results, clearly shown.
